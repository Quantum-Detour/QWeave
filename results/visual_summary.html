⚡ 2. Circuit Operation Flow & Execution Phases

All methods prepare the Bell state |Φ⁺⟩ from all-zero physical inputs across designated ports. Method D (QMRP) inserts a 2-qubit repetition encoding and extraction block protected by phase barriers:

Phase 1: Local Bell Prep

Applies H(R) and CX(R, launch_A) locally on adjacent sites without zero-state prepositioning.

Phase 2: Repetition Encoding

Encodes traveling qubit across two physical rails using local CX(launch_A, launch_B).

Phase 3: Disjoint Transport

Moves the two rails outward along capacity-constrained vertex-disjoint paths discovered via min-cost flow.

Phase 4: Decoding & Parity

Applies CX(D, S) at destination, extracting pre-decoding Z_a Z_b parity into syndrome qubit S.

Phase 5: Basis Rotation

Rotates ports into XX (H), YY (Sdg + H), or ZZ basis, measuring S in Z and postselecting on S=0.
